Inside Sangam Setu

Four tabs, each with one job

Home: matches from your community

Home shows profiles of the opposite gender. Filter them by age, city and community, and every card shows who is verified.

  • Filter by age range, state and city, and community
  • Save a profile to come back to it later
  • Until you're verified, Send Request shows a lock and a link to verify

Request: received and sent

Received requests come with a one-line introduction, so you can decide quickly. Sent requests show their status in words.

  • Accept or decline each received request
  • See whether each sent request is pending, accepted or declined
  • Withdraw a request that is still pending

Connection: everyone who said yes

When a request is accepted, both sides see each other's contact number so the families can speak directly.

  • The contact person's name and number on every card
  • Shows who you're calling, such as a father or mother
  • Addresses still show only the city and state

Profile: your details in one list

See your profile the way others see it, edit any section, and check your verification status.

  • Edit details, work, family and address
  • Verification status and date
  • Change who takes the calls without editing everything else

Questions

Families usually ask us

Who can see our address?
Only your city and state, for example "Indore, Madhya Pradesh". Your house number, street and pincode are never shown to anyone, even after a request is accepted.Sangam Setu
When is our phone number shared?
Your contact person's number is shared only with people whose request you accept, or who accept yours. Both sides see each other's number at the same time. Until then it stays hidden.Sangam Setu
Why is Aadhaar needed?
Verification is required to send or accept requests, so everyone you connect with has been checked. You upload the front and back of the card. Only our verification team sees it, and it's never shown on your profile.Sangam Setu
Can we look at profiles before verifying?
Yes. As soon as your profile is live you can browse, filter and save profiles. Verification is needed only when you want to send or accept a request.Sangam Setu
Which profiles will we see?
Profiles of the opposite gender. You can narrow them by age range, state and city, and community, and choose to see only verified profiles.Sangam Setu
Can parents manage the conversations?
Yes. Choose a parent, sibling or anyone else as the contact person. Their number is the one shared after a request is accepted, and you can change it any time from your profile.Sangam Setu
Is there chat inside Sangam Setu?
No. Once a request is accepted, both sides see each other's contact person and number, and the families talk directly by phone.Sangam Setu
Do we need a password?
No. You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code, so there's no password to forget.Sangam Setu
