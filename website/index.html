Privacy by design

Your family's details stay private until you say yes

A matrimony profile asks for personal details. These rules decide who sees them and when, and they apply to every profile.

  • One mobile number gets you in

    Sign in with your number and a 4-digit OTP. There are no passwords to remember or reset.

  • Browse freely, connect when verified

    Once your profile is live you can look at every match. Sending or accepting a request needs an Aadhaar-verified account, so everyone you hear from has been checked.

  • Addresses stop at the city

    Other people see your city and state only. Your house number, street and pincode are never shown on your profile.

  • Numbers are shared only after acceptance, and both ways

    A contact number appears only when a request is accepted, and then both sides see each other's. Accepting shares a phone number. It never shares a home address.

Inside the app

Four tabs, each with one job

Home: matches from your community

Home shows profiles of the opposite gender. Filter them by age, city and community, and every card tells you who is verified.

  • Filter by age range, state and city, and community
  • Save a profile to come back to it later
  • Until you're verified, Send Request shows a lock and a link to verify

Request: received and sent

Requests you've received come with a short introduction, so you can decide quickly. Requests you've sent show their status in words.

  • Accept or decline each received request
  • See whether each sent request is pending, accepted or declined
  • Withdraw a request that is still pending

Connection: everyone who said yes

There's no in-app chat. When a request is accepted, both sides see each other's contact number so the families can speak directly.

  • The contact person's name and number on every card
  • Shows who you're calling, such as a father or mother
  • Addresses still show only the city and state

Profile: your details in one list

See your profile the way others see it, edit any section, and check your verification status.

  • Edit details, work, family and address
  • Verification status and date
  • Change who takes the calls without editing everything else

Questions

Common questions

What people usually ask before they create a profile.

Who can see my address?

Only your city and state are shown on your profile, for example "Indore, Madhya Pradesh". Your house number, street and pincode are never shown to anyone, even after a request is accepted.

When is my phone number shared?

Your contact person's number is shared only with people whose request you accept, or who accept yours. Both sides see each other's number at the same time. Until then it stays hidden.

Why do I need to verify with Aadhaar?

Verification is required to send or accept requests, so everyone you connect with has been checked. You upload the front and back of your card. Only our verification team sees it, and it is never shown on your profile.

Can I look at profiles before I'm verified?

Yes. As soon as your profile is live you can browse, filter and save profiles. You'll need to verify only when you want to send a request or accept one.

Which profiles will I see?

Home shows profiles of the opposite gender. You can narrow them by age range, state and city, and community, and choose to see only verified profiles.

Can my parents manage the conversations?

Yes. Choose a parent, sibling or anyone else as your contact person while building your profile. Their number is the one shared after a request is accepted, and you can change it at any time from the Profile tab.

Is there chat in the app?

No. Once a request is accepted, both sides see each other's contact person and number, and the families talk directly by phone.

Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code, so there's no password to forget or reset.
